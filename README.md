## So What Actually Is "Context Charting"?

Think of it as a more practical evolution of mind mapping. Traditional mind maps are great for brainstorming but leave you stranded when it's time to actually do something with the ideas. Context charting takes the visual structure of a mind map and wraps it with live research: as you build your map, Contextminds automatically pulls in related keywords, popular search questions, top-ranking article headlines, and even academic papers.

You're not just organizing thoughts — you're doing research and organization at the same time, in one place.

The platform is built around three components that actually work together:

- **A whiteboard** where you drag, arrange, and annotate all your ideas
- **A search engine** that automatically surfaces related keywords, web results, and questions as you work
- **GPT integration** that uses your entire whiteboard as a prompt to generate drafts, outlines, or summaries in one click

That third piece is the key. Most AI writing tools suffer from "garbage in, garbage out." Contextminds solves the problem at the source — by making the prompt itself rich and structured before you ever ask the AI to write anything.
